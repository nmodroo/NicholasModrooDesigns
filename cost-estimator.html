Cost Estimator FAQ

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. Your first consultation and an initial site visit are complimentary — a chance to walk the land, talk through your goals, and see whether we're the right fit, with no obligation. A full Pre-Purchase Site Selection & Feasibility Study is a separate, deeper engagement for when you're evaluating a specific parcel in detail.

How accurate is this estimate?

It's a preliminary, order-of-magnitude range for early planning — not a bid. Real costs are set through site analysis, design development, and builder pricing. Think of it as the starting point for that conversation.

What's included, and what isn't?

Included: residence construction, sitework and utilities, design, engineering and project management, and a recommended owner contingency. Not included: the land itself, interiors and furnishings, and financing. The cost per square foot is all-in — everything above divided by the home's size. Builder quotes per square foot usually cover construction alone, so that figure is shown separately for a like-for-like comparison.

Why does location change the number so much?

Resort and remote markets carry higher labor and material costs, mobilization for crews and equipment, and longer design-review and permitting timelines. Steep or slope-side sites add foundation, excavation and snow-load engineering on top of that.

What happens after I send my profile?

Your selections arrive with your message, so Nicholas can review your site, scale and goals and follow up personally with a more grounded conversation about budget and timeline.
