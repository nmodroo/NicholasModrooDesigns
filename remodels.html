The Work

Honor the Bones, Elevate Everything

Not every great mountain home needs to start from scratch. Sometimes the site is perfect, the structure is sound, and what's missing is the design vision to unlock the home's potential — better light, a more natural flow, a stronger connection to the views, and spaces that finally match how you live.

That's the heart of a remodel. We approach renovations and additions with the same rigor we bring to ground-up custom homes: understanding what's worth preserving, where the opportunities are, and how to weave new work into the existing structure so the result feels intentional rather than added-on.

Our remodel of the Rock Creek log cabin in Red Lodge — a creekside residence reimagined for modern mountain living — is a good example of how a thoughtful renovation can completely transform how a home feels and functions while respecting its character.

Common Questions

Remodel FAQ

Do you take on whole-home remodels as well as additions?

Yes. We handle everything from focused additions to full gut renovations and complete reimaginings of an existing home. No project is too complex.

Can you remodel a home in a resort community like Big Sky?

Absolutely. We have deep experience with design review and ARB approvals at Big Sky, Yellowstone Club, and Moonlight Basin — often the trickiest part of remodeling in a resort community.

How do remodels differ from a new custom home?

Remodels reward a designer who can read an existing structure — preserving what works, identifying hidden opportunities, and integrating new work seamlessly. We bring the same design rigor as a ground-up home, adapted to the realities of building within what's already there.

What areas do you serve for remodels?

We're based in Bozeman, MT and design remodels across the mountain west — Big Sky, Red Lodge, Livingston, Teton Valley, and throughout Montana, Wyoming and Idaho.
