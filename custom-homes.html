The Work

A Home as Precise as It Is Personal

A custom home is the most personal thing most families will ever build. For more than twenty years, Nicholas Modroo Designs has guided clients through that process across the mountain west — designing homes that are shaped by their site, their views, and the lives that will unfold within them.

We work across the full spectrum of mountain living: sculptural mountain modern homes of glass and steel, warmly textured traditional log and timber, and everything between. Whatever the style, the discipline is the same — every massing decision, view corridor, and material is chosen to make the finished home feel inevitable.

Crucially, we don't hand you a set of drawings and walk away. As designer and owner's representative, we stay engaged through documentation, permitting, and construction administration — so the home you imagined is the home that actually gets built.

Common Questions

Custom Home FAQ

How much does it cost to build a custom home in Montana?

Costs vary widely with site, size, finish level, and resort requirements. We establish a realistic budget early in the discovery phase and design to it — so the home you fall in love with on paper is the home you can actually build.

How long does a custom home take?

Most custom homes run roughly 18 to 36 months from first sketch to move-in, depending on size, site, design-review requirements, and the builder's schedule. We map a realistic timeline up front and manage it throughout.

Where do you design custom homes?

We're based in Bozeman, MT and work across the mountain west — Big Sky, Yellowstone Club, Moonlight Basin, Teton Valley, and throughout Montana, Wyoming, Idaho, Colorado, Utah and Alaska.

Do you also manage construction?

Yes. We serve as both designer and owner's representative, coordinating architects, engineers, builders, and interior designers from first sketch through construction close-out.
