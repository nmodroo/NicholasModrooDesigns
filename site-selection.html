The Work

The Most Important Decision Comes First

Long before the first sketch, one decision shapes everything that follows: the land. The right parcel makes a home feel inevitable. The wrong one quietly adds cost, complication, and compromise to every phase that comes after — sometimes in ways that aren't obvious until it's too late to change them.

That's why it pays to involve a designer before you buy. We walk a property with a builder's and a designer's eye, reading the slope, the soils, the sun, the views, and the access — and we translate what we see into the practical realities of what it will take to build there, and what it will cost.

The result is confidence. You make an offer knowing what the land can become, where the constraints are, and how your future home will sit on it.

Common Questions

Site Selection FAQ

Can you evaluate a lot before I make an offer?

Yes — that's when our input is most valuable. We assess a parcel's buildability and constraints before you commit, so you buy with clear eyes and avoid costly surprises later.

What do you look for in a building site?

Buildability and slope, view corridors and solar orientation, access and utilities, and any ARB, covenant or zoning constraints — plus how each of these affects construction cost.

Do you work alongside my real estate agent?

Absolutely. We complement your agent by assessing the design and construction realities of a parcel, giving you and your agent a fuller picture before an offer.

Where do you offer site consulting?

Across the mountain west — Bozeman, Big Sky, Yellowstone Club, Teton Valley, and throughout Montana, Wyoming and Idaho.

Thinking About a Parcel?

Before you make an offer, let's walk the land together — on site or on a map — and talk through what it could become.
