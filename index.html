The Studio

Design that
Belongs to Here

Nicholas Modroo Designs is a residential design studio rooted in the mountain west — based in Bozeman, MT and working across Big Sky, Yellowstone Club, Teton Valley, and beyond.

Led by Nicholas Modroo, the studio serves as designer and project manager from first sketch through construction close-out — ensuring a seamless journey and a home that is as precisely built as it is carefully imagined.

Our work spans the full spectrum of mountain living: from sculptural mountain modern glass and steel to warmly textured traditional log — every home shaped by its site, its views, and the singular vision of those who will inhabit it.

What We Offer

Full-Spectrum
Design Services

◈
Custom Estates

Ground-up custom homes from 3,000 to 15,000+ sq ft. We guide every decision from site analysis and massing through material selection, construction documentation, and administration.

◧
Remodels & Additions

Transformative renovations that honor the bones of an existing home while elevating its functionality, flow, and connection to the landscape. No project too complex.

◉
Project Management

Acting as owner's representative and PM, we coordinate architects, engineers, builders, and interior designers — keeping your project on schedule, on budget, and on vision.

◬
Mountain Modern

Our signature style — clean lines, natural stone and steel, expansive glass that dissolves the boundary between inside and the mountain panorama beyond.

◰
Traditional Log & Timber

Authentic log and heavy timber homes with the warmth of hand-hewn craftsmanship — from intimate creek-side cabins to grand ski lodges and heli-ski retreats.

◲
Ski-In / Ski-Out

Specialized expertise in resort community design at Yellowstone Club, Moonlight Basin, and beyond — navigating design review, ARB compliance, and slope-side siting.

How We Work

A Seamless Journey
from Concept to Keys

01
Discovery

Site visits, vision sessions, budget alignment, and program development to understand exactly what you need from your home.

02
Design

Concept massing, schematic design, and design development — iterating until every room, view corridor, and material tells your story.

03
Documentation

Construction documents, ARB submittals, permit drawings, and full coordination with structural and MEP engineers.

04
Administration

On-site presence, RFI management, change order review, and punch list close-out — we stay until the last detail is right.
