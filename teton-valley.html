The Project

Bold Enough for the Tetons

In the shadow of the Tetons, a home has to hold its own against one of the most dramatic skylines in the country. This 13,200-square-foot residence and guest house answers that scale with architecture that is confident and precise — bold massing, deep covered outdoor rooms, and walls of glass that pull the range straight inside.

Nicholas Modroo served as designer and project manager across the full arc of the project, from the earliest design concept through construction administration and punch-list close-out. A home of this size and ambition lives or dies on coordination, and ours stayed seamless from first sketch to final detail.

The finished estate balances grandeur with genuine warmth — generous for entertaining, intimate where it counts, and rooted unmistakably in its place.
