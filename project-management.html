The Work

One Person Holding It All Together

A custom home is a long, complex undertaking with a lot of moving parts — an architect, structural and civil engineers, a builder, subcontractors, an interior designer, a landscape architect, and a review board, all working over many months. Without someone holding the whole picture, details slip, costs creep, and the design intent quietly erodes.

That's the role we play. As your owner's representative and project manager, we sit on your side of the table from first sketch through construction close-out — coordinating the team, keeping the schedule honest, reviewing every budget and change order, and making sure the home that gets built is the home that was designed.

The builder still builds. The architect still designs. But you have one advocate making sure all of it serves your interests — so the process feels seamless instead of overwhelming.

Common Questions

Project Management FAQ

What does an owner's representative do?

An owner's representative is your single advocate on the project — coordinating the design and construction team, reviewing budgets and change orders, and protecting your interests so you don't have to manage a dozen relationships yourself.

Do you replace the general contractor?

No. The builder still builds. We sit on your side of the table — coordinating the team, holding the schedule and budget accountable, and making sure the home is built to the design intent.

When should I bring you in?

As early as possible — ideally at concept. The earlier we're involved, the more we can align team, schedule and budget before decisions become expensive to change. Start with a preliminary investment range →

Where do you manage projects?

Across the mountain west — Bozeman, Big Sky, Yellowstone Club, Moonlight Basin, and throughout Montana, Wyoming and Idaho.
